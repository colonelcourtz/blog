Hello world

This is my first post in what I hope to maintain as an ongoing record of the daft little projects I work on in my spare time.

Over the past few years, I’ve found myself working on increasingly varied projects with no particular purpose beyond the joy of creating the thing itself, and the learning that inevitably happens somewhere along the way.

Following my latest foray into the previously (for me) uncharted territory of embedded development, an esteemed colleague and friend asked me a perfectly reasonable question:

`"What was my plan when I’d finished it?"`

I had no answer.

What was I doing this for? Who was I doing it for? Why was I even doing it?

As a new father, I already have remarkably little time to do the necessary things in life, never mind voluntarily introducing a series of completely pointless engineering projects into the equation. The irony of addressing this shortage of time by starting a blog is not lost on me.

And yet, here we are.

Perhaps these projects aren’t entirely pointless. They give me an excuse to learn things I would otherwise never have a reason to learn, to venture confidently into technical disciplines in which I have absolutely no business venturing, and occasionally to produce something that actually works.

So this blog is an attempt to document that process.

Not necessarily as tutorials. Certainly not as examples of best practice. More as a record of what happens when curiosity, questionable judgement and access to the internet are given sufficient time to interact.

Consider it a contribution to the emerging field of Applied Bodgery.
