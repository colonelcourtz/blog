As a developer there comes a time when a project makes its transition from development to production. It's a terrifying moment and often comes with a mix of celebration, nervousness and panic when something suddenly happens which never happened in testing and we need to franticly patch some bugs.

In this post I'd like to focus on the celebration aspect, and pretend the other bits don't happen. More specifically I'd like to focus on a little project I embarked upon to mark the occasion.

A few weeks before the set launch date as a team we discussed what we might to do mark the unveiling of our years long project on the world. Some mentioned food (a classic according to the wonderful Ologies podcast on (Party Psychology)[https://www.alieward.com/ologies/partypsychology]), others mentioned drink, then one offhand comment rose above the chatter for a second (I can't actually remember who said it), but someone said "Oh, wouldn't it be cool if we could press a big red button to launch it". 

Well, that's all my brain needed to jump into action and dedicate all it's spare time and processing power to working on just that.

A one-time, completely pointless, completely unnecessary, surprisingly time-consuming project which at the end would be shelved away as junk.

Perfect! Just what I like.
